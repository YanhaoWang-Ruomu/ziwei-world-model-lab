Cultural association scheme used by this edition:
https://zh.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=南斗六星&oldid=94291277
Tianfu (order 1) -> Dipper VI; Tianliang (2) -> Dipper V;
Tianji (3) -> Dipper IV; Tiantong (4) -> Dipper I;
Tianxiang (5) -> Dipper II; Qisha (6) -> Dipper III.
This is the cited table's scheme, not a universally agreed physical identification.
Astronomical numbering, Ziwei terminology and Daoist six-office deity ordering
must not be silently substituted for one another. For example, the deity order in
https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/太上說南斗六司延壽度人妙經
differs from the Ziwei order displayed here.
No cultural association modifies astrometric coordinates or chart calculations.
